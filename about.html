Linh

"I'm a content and insights analyst based in Houston, TX — though I got my start somewhere a lot less glamorous: warehouse logistics."

"Most of my working life has been spent finding the pattern in messy, real-world data and turning it into something people actually use — whether that's fixing a manual labeling process that was quietly corrupting production reports, or digging through consumer reviews to test whether a marketing claim actually holds up. I like the part of analysis most people skip: checking whether the data can be trusted before deciding what it means."

"I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from Seattle University, and I'm currently finishing an M.S. in Business Analytics there too. Different titles along the way, same underlying instinct: find what's actually true in the data, then explain it clearly to someone who needs to act on it."
